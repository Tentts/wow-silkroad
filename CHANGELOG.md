# SilkRoad 0.7.0

The first public release of SilkRoad, a crafting and trading companion for World of
Warcraft: Forever.

## Features

- **Recipes for every profession**: all 2,129 recipes of the 12 professions, with material
  cost, raw cost (crafting every step yourself), auction and vendor prices, profits, and
  whether to sell to the auction house or a vendor. Known recipes are marked automatically.
- **Where to get recipes**: trainers, vendors, drops, quests and containers, with zones,
  coordinates and map waypoints (Questie recommended).
- **Leveling Guide**: the cheapest route to level a profession from current prices, with a
  shopping list, training reminders and progress tracking. Gathering professions get
  zone guides with map waypoints.
- **Buy lists**: build shopping lists from any recipe, then buy them item by item from the
  new Buy Lists tab in the auction house. Every purchase needs your click and confirmation.
- **Automatic auction house scans** that respect the server's 15-minute limit, and share
  data with Auctionator.
- **Prices in item tooltips**, a Price Database page, and merchant prices recorded as you
  visit vendors.
- **Recipe tracking** in the objective tracker from a recipe's right-click menu.
- Minimap button, key binding, addon compartment entry and an About page.
- Available in **English** and **Spanish**.
