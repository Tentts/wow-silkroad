# SilkRoad 0.7.7

## New

- **Full bags while looting** (optional, off by default): turn on "Show what to throw away
  when bags are full" in Settings > General. When looting fails because your bags are full,
  the cheapest stacks in your bags that are worth less than the loot glow red for a few
  seconds, and chat lists them with their value, so you know what to throw away. Items
  without a known value, such as quest items, are never suggested. Works with Blizzard's
  bags and with Baganator.
