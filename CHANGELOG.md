# SilkRoad 0.7.5

## Fixed

- **Buy lists**: removing an item from its right-click menu (or setting its amount to 0)
  removed the last item of the list instead of the one you clicked.
- **Leveling Guide**: training reminders now use Forever's trainer requirements, Journeyman at
  75, Expert at 150 and Artisan at 225, instead of 25 points earlier as in Classic.
